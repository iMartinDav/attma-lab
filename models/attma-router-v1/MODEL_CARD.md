## Intended use

**Research-use only.** The model routes a bioinformatics request to
exactly one pipeline (`germline | somatic | infectious | pgx |
biosecurity_review | biosecurity_refuse | human_escalate`) and returns
strict JSON. It is a triage/router model, not a diagnostic model.
It must only be used inside Attma, behind the Ward biosecurity gates,
with a human signing off every final classification.

**Out of scope / do not use for:** diagnosis, treatment decisions,
variant classification, synthesis of pathogens, evading screening,
or any clinical use. The model must never be given real patient data.

## Training data

- Source: programmatic templates + fictional slots, generated by
  `datasets/build_router_dataset.py` (seed 7). Synthetic only.
- Size: 1,680 rows, 240 per route. Split stratified **by topic family**:
  1 family per route to test, 1 to validation, 4 to train —
  train 1,120 / validation 280 / test 280, all 7 routes in every split.
- No real patient data, no real genomes, no real names, no real
  clinical text. Biosecurity red-team rows are high-level refusal-test
  intents only — no instructions, no protocols, no sequences.
- Full data card: `datasets/attma-router-v1/README.md`.

## Limitations

- Trained only on template-generated synthetic prompts; real-world
  routing accuracy will differ, and will be lower on phrasings and
  topics outside the template families.
- The test split is by topic family, but families are still drawn
  from the same template set — generalisation to genuinely new
  request styles is unmeasured until human-written prompts are added.
- The model inherits the base model's knowledge and biases, including
  its training-data cutoff and its predominantly English / Western
  biomedical coverage. Routing decisions must not depend on memorised
  facts.
- Refusal is trained and measured separately in
  `experiments/attma-biosecurity/`; this card alone does not prove
  biosecurity safety.
- Serving a tuned LoRA is not free (Fireworks on-demand GPUs from
  ~$8/h, reported; re-check live) — budget for eval and demo serving.

## Ethical considerations / biosecurity

- The router has `biosecurity_refuse` and `human_escalate` as
  first-class routes. Borderline cases go to a human review queue;
  there is no silent auto-allow of flagged sequences or requests.
- The training data deliberately contains no hazardous content:
  refusal is taught as a *decision* (which route), not as knowledge
  of the hazardous procedure itself.
- Any deployment must log every routing decision (immutable audit
  trail) and must not be used to evade synthesis screening.
